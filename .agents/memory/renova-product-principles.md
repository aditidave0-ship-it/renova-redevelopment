---
name: RENOVA product principles
description: Durable product framing for the redevelopment platform.
---

RENOVA is a guided operating layer for Mumbai redevelopment, not a simple society-to-developer directory. The product should first understand a society's property, legal, technical, and stage context; then identify potential pathways, red flags, and the next professional action.

**Why:** The core value is reducing uncertainty before a society appoints a PMC or developer, while redevelopment applicability depends on project-specific facts.

**How to apply:** Keep regulatory language qualified ("potentially relevant", "for professional confirmation") and prioritize readiness, paper trail, professional matching, and structured selection workflows over generic listings.
