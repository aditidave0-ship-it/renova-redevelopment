I researched the current Mumbai redevelopment framework before answering. One important correction first:

> **For Mumbai, the governing framework you should build around is DCPR 2034, not “DCPR 2035.”**

The official BMC DCPR document is the **Development Control and Promotion Regulations 2034 for Greater Mumbai**. It has multiple redevelopment-specific regulations, and the applicable route depends heavily on the nature of the land/building. 

For example, MHADA has specific redevelopment provisions under **Regulation 33(5)**; cessed buildings involve **33(7)**; other old/tenant-occupied buildings can fall under **33(7A)/(7B)**; cluster redevelopment is addressed under **33(9)**; and SRA schemes operate under provisions including **33(10) and 33(11)**. ([Mhada][1])

**Society → Developer**

**Society → RENOVA → Assessment → PMC → Tender/EOI → Developers → Evaluation → Selection → Regulatory/Project Journey**

And RENOVA sits in the **middle as the organiser, connector and technology layer**.
